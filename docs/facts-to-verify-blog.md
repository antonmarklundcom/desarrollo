# Facts to verify — blog phase

Articles state these in general terms and tell the reader to check the value in force. Check them before launch:

- Salary ranges in `sueldo-de-un-programador-en-paraguay` are described only as multiples of the minimum wage (junior: somewhat above the minimum up to about 2x). Check against current trabajo.com.py listings and the current minimum wage.
- SIFEN: the rollout by designated groups, the KuDE with a QR code, the CDC code, the document types (factura, nota de crédito, nota de débito, nota de remisión, autofactura), the free DNIT tool for low volume, and the contingency procedures (`facturacion-electronica-obligatoria-que-cambia-para-su-empresa`).
- The name of the ekuatia portal, and the costs and steps for the digital certificate and for signing up as an electronic invoicer.
- WhatsApp Business API: the per-conversation or per-message charges by category, and the rule that templates are required outside the service window (`whatsapp-para-vender-mas`).
- Payments: that Bancard, Pagopar and Tigo Money are still active, which payment methods they cover, their fees and settlement times, and whether they support recurring charges (`como-cobrar-online-en-paraguay`).
- Tax treatment for remote programmers who bill clients abroad: service exports, VAT and personal income tax (IRP) (`trabajo-remoto-para-programadores-paraguayos`).
- The legal framework for internships (`pasantias-de-programacion-en-paraguay`).
- Universities are named in general terms only (UNA, UCA, UAA). The article gives no study plans, fees or program lengths.
